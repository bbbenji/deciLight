Pin numbers below are ESP32 GPIO numbers, not board silkscreen labels.
On a FireBeetle the two differ - its "D2" pad is GPIO25, not GPIO2.

Signal (Y)  GPIO2

MIC (INMP441 / ICS-43434)

I2S WS (B)  GPIO15
I2S SCK (G) GPIO14
L/R         GND

IR (VS1838B)
Signal (Y)  GPIO4

OLED (SSD1306 128x64, I2C)   -- wired, not yet driven by the firmware
